# mySchlawgs

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to share everyday photos and stories of their companion animals and connect with other animal owners and enthusiasts.

## Product Purpose

A welcoming community for sharing animal moments, browsing posts, and connecting through reactions and comments. All companion animals are welcome.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing app uses Next.js and React for the frontend, ASP.NET Core for the backend, and PostgreSQL for storage. Existing features include accounts, photo posts, animal profiles, reactions, comments, notifications, and moderation tools.

Design work must preserve existing functionality and accurate product information unless a change is explicitly requested. Do not invent testimonials, usage statistics, or other evidence.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the mySchlawgs name and its welcoming, playful community voice. Animal care and kindness guide the community.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing application, README.md, and frontend/app/our-mission/page.tsx document current workflows and community principles. The user confirmed the audience and purpose above during Impeccable setup.

## Product Principles

- Let people share everyday animal moments comfortably.
- Welcome all companion animals.
- Make browsing, sharing, and community interaction easy to understand.
- Put animal care and kindness first.
